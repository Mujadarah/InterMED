# Interaction data source assessment — Milestone 9

Assessment date: **2026-10-12**. Status: **research proposal; no provider approved**.

## Decision and scope

No candidate is established here as both clinically sufficient for Romania and licensed for public distribution of interaction data to offline devices. The recommended order for clarification and evaluation is **DDInter 2.0, Stockley's Interactions Checker, then DrugBank Clinical**. These are conditional candidates, not an approved production selection. A small non-commercial app still needs explicit rights for its actual distribution and clinical use.

This assessment addresses the M9 source-quality/licence/cost/offline-rights task in [CODEX_BUILD_PLAN](../plan/CODEX_BUILD_PLAN.md). It does not close M9 clinical acceptance or the M10 release gate. Read it with [DATA_SOURCES](../plan/DATA_SOURCES.md), [INTERACTIONS](../plan/INTERACTIONS.md), and [CLINICAL_SAFETY](../plan/CLINICAL_SAFETY.md). The owner must record an approved provider decision, permitted use, clinician review and operational controls before M10 release.

Public primary documentation was researched; licence, terms and pricing claims were checked against provider pages where accessible. No licensed interaction dataset was imported, no clinical rule was generated, and no vendor was contacted. Public claims about editorial quality are provider claims, not independent clinical validation. **Unverified** means the reviewed material did not establish the fact; it does not mean the feature or permission cannot exist. A missing public API is not proof that a private licensed feed is unavailable.

The free CLI worker supplied raw DDInter/Liverpool/ONC research outside the repository. Its conclusions were qualified where necessary: a failed search cannot prove that an ONC resource no longer exists; traffic-light risk is not evidence strength; and DDInter's CC licence must be distinguished from its additional personal-use wording. The commercial CLI worker stalled without findings; those sources were researched directly.

**Cost convention:** no verified public tariff for third-party offline DDI redistribution was found for any commercial candidate. “Quote required” below is not an estimated price, nor proof that a vendor offers the required deal. Individual subscriptions, trials, free websites, academic research licences and a provider's own offline app do not establish permission to embed its data in InterMED.

## Clinical content, identifiers and Romania fit

### Identity and terminology policy

For each candidate, “INN/generic names” describes naming content, not proof of a complete authoritative INN crosswalk. ATC is a classification: route, indication and combination products can affect the code; it is not a unique active-ingredient key. [WHO ATC principles](https://atcddd.fhi.no/atc/structure_and_principles/) support this distinction. RxNorm and UNII mappings must be checked for the delivered product, version, salts, combinations and local scope; a vendor offering one identifier elsewhere does not prove it is present in its DDI feed.

InterMED needs a reviewed mapping from Romanian CIM/DCI products to all component ingredients, then to licensed provider IDs. Preserve source/version, unmapped products, route/dose qualifiers and mapping uncertainty; never silently remap an ambiguous ingredient. Severity, recommended action, evidence strength and confidence in mapping are different fields. Keep native vocabulary alongside a clinician-approved mapping to InterMED's controlled values. An absent pair must remain “not assessed/no record”, never “safe”.

### DrugBank Clinical

**Content and identifiers:** descriptive interactions and structured, directional DDI records are distinct products/tables. The CSV documentation exposes severity, evidence, management and references, with DrugBank IDs, ATC, UNII and RxNorm in specified tables; verify the purchased schema and cross-table joins. **Vocabulary:** structured severity includes minor/moderate/major; evidence uses a documented numeric field, not a universal evidence scale. Obtain the full current dictionary. [CSV documentation](https://docs.drugbank.com/csv/).

**Cadence and fit:** the release page currently identifies 5.1.22, dated 2026-06-27; that is an observed release, not a delivery SLA. Clinical materials advertise EU/US/Canada regulatory coverage. Ingredient-level coverage is promising for a Romanian crosswalk; complete local products and Romanian text are unverified. Distinguish scientific breadth from independent clinical validation. Academic research access is not public clinical redistribution. [Release](https://go.drugbank.com/releases/latest), [Clinical product](https://go.drugbank.com/clinical/), [terms](https://trust.drugbank.com/drugbank-trust-center/terms-of-use).

### FDB MedKnowledge

**Content:** configurable DDI module with severity/category filtering and professional monographs. [Module](https://www.fdbhealth.com/solutions/medknowledge-drug-database/medknowledge-clinical-modules/drug-drug-interaction).

**Unverified:** delivered ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII crosswalk, exact severity/evidence dictionary, update SLA, Romanian coverage and language. Evaluate regional products separately. [UK portfolio](https://www.fdbhealth.co.uk/).

**Assessment:** reserve candidate pending a representative Romanian sample and explicit device rights. Website access does not authorize redistribution. [Terms](https://www.fdbhealth.com/terms-of-use).

### Medi-Span

**Content and identifiers:** Drug Therapy Monitoring System content includes DDI/food/alcohol rules with severity, management and documentation; content sets have flat-file/API/web-service delivery options. Proprietary GPI/DDID identifiers appear in the portfolio. ATC, formal INN, RxNorm and UNII coverage of the actual DDI deliverable is unverified; do not infer it from separate products. [Content sets](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/medi-span/medi-span/content-sets).

**Vocabulary/cadence:** obtain the current severity and documentation-level dictionary. Editorial material describes multidisciplinary review and daily surveillance, which does not establish a daily offline snapshot SLA. [Editorial process](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/medi-span/about/content-and-editorial-process).

**Romania/quality:** professional curated content is a credible evaluation candidate. US-oriented medication files do not prove EU brand, ingredient or Romanian-language completeness. Require a Romanian ingredient sample, rules for combinations/routes, regional licence and device-update terms before comparison on clinical coverage.

### Lexicomp / UpToDate Lexidrug

**Content:** clinician-facing interaction analysis with referenced management and contextual qualifiers. **Vocabulary:** risk ratings A (no known interaction), B (no action), C (monitor), D (consider therapy modification), X (avoid combination) describe action/risk, not a separate evidence-strength grade. Exact evidence dictionary, DDI feed identifiers (ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII), delivery cadence and Romanian localization are unverified. [Clinical modules](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/uptodate/resources/lexidrug-user-academy/clinical-modules).

**Fit/limitations:** useful professional comparator if licensed for evaluation, but a subscription/app is not a redistributable ingredient-pair feed. Require separate integration and translation permission. End-user app restrictions are material even when InterMED is free. [App terms](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/uptodate/policies-legal/uptodate-lexidrug-disclaimer-apps).

### Stockley's Interactions Checker / Drug Interactions

**Content:** the checker provides structured interaction summaries linked to fuller monographs, covering international medicines and literature/case evidence. Native reactant IDs/names are available in the documented API; ATC, formal INN, RxNorm and UNII export mappings are unverified. **Vocabulary:** severity, action and evidence are separate ratings. Public API examples include Severe, Avoid and Theoretical; these examples are not a complete enum dictionary. Obtain all values, class-expansion rules and qualifiers. [Checker](https://www.pharmaceuticalpress.com/content/stockleys-interactions-checker/), [monographs](https://www.pharmaceuticalpress.com/content/stockleys-drug-interactions/), [API documentation](https://content.medicinescomplete.com/docs/index.html).

**Cadence/quality:** monthly updates are advertised. Pharmacist assessment and linked monographs make the checker a strong clinical evaluation candidate, but these are provider descriptions, not Romanian validation. International coverage is promising; Romanian ingredients, language and licensed translation are unverified. The documented REST integration may require connectivity unless a separate snapshot licence is negotiated. Public website terms restrict permanent storage and translation; the separate data-licensing offer is the route to resolve this. [Licensing](https://www.pharmaceuticalpress.com/data-licensing/applications/), [terms](https://www.pharmaceuticalpress.com/terms-and-conditions/).

This transparent policy is a strength; selective inclusion means no record cannot be treated as proof of no interaction. **Vocabulary:** exact current severity/documentation enums and their definitions were not verified from a current licensed dictionary. ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII export coverage is also unverified. [DDI policy](https://assets.merative.com/m/4ca06ad3981a94ab/original/MDX_Drug_Interactions_Policy.pdf).

**Cadence/fit:** daily updates and provider offline capabilities are advertised, without establishing third-party offline delivery rights. EU/Romanian ingredient coverage, Romanian content and licensed feed update/expiry obligations require confirmation. [Solution brief](https://assets.merative.com/m/279c89b761b2a467/original/Micromedex_MicromedexOverview_Solution-Brief.pdf). The linked [service description](https://www.merative.com/content/dam/merative/terms/offering/service/Micromedex_Service_Description.pdf) could not be directly verified in this assessment; no grant is inferred from it. Request the controlling agreement and a representative sample.

### Medscape

**Content/vocabulary:** public interaction checker with management-oriented categories including contraindicated, serious/use alternative, significant/monitor closely and minor. It is an end-user reference, not a verified bulk feed. A stable schema, independent evidence-strength dictionary, update cadence and ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII export are unverified. [Checker](https://reference.medscape.com/drug-interactionchecker).

**Fit/limitations:** useful as a human-accessible reference subject to its terms, but no verified Romanian content, complete EU coverage or third-party integration rights. Free access does not justify scraping, caching or republishing. Its [terms](https://www.medscape.com/public/termsofuse) were verified through direct public-page retrieval when the research browser could not retrieve them; they restrict copying/derivatives/distribution beyond the limited access grant. No permitted offline InterMED acquisition route was established.

### ONC high-priority DDI list

**Content/quality:** the ONC-commissioned Phansalkar et al. 2012 work selected 15 high-priority DDI categories for EHR decision support through expert consensus. Categories include drug/class relationships; they are not necessarily 15 simple normalized ingredient pairs. This is a deliberately narrow high-priority reference, not a comprehensive interaction knowledge base. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1136/amiajnl-2011-000612), [bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22539083/).

**Vocabulary/identifiers/cadence:** high-priority selection is not a graded current severity/evidence dataset. A maintained ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII crosswalk, modern release schedule and official current machine-readable distribution were not verified. Literature publication date is not a feed freshness guarantee. Government commissioning does not resolve rights in article tables or third-party terminology.

**Romania/limits:** historic US EHR priorities can inform a clinician-reviewed validation checklist if rights permit. They cannot establish EU coverage, Romanian language, a negative interaction result or a production source. Bounded searches not finding a current ONC download do not establish its absence. Obtain provenance and licence for the specific list artifact before reusing it.

### DDInter 2.0

**Content and identifiers:** curated drug-pair records with mechanism, management and citations, native drug IDs/names and ATC classification. Formal INN completeness and RxNorm/UNII mappings are unverified. The current site describes pharmacist input and literature/label sources; record counts differ between some pages, so they are not used as a completeness measure. [Current site](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/), [explanation](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/explanation/), [statistics](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/statistics/).

**Vocabulary/cadence:** Major, Moderate, Minor, Unknown; references do not constitute a verified separate evidence-strength grading system. The site still displays last update **2024-05-14**. No dependable continuing update SLA or current 2.0 bulk-download channel was verified; legacy 1.0 downloads must not be passed off as 2.0. These are material freshness and acquisition blockers.

**Rights/fit:** the explicit data licence is an advantage for a non-commercial proposal, but does not establish clinical adequacy. The additional personal-use wording and upstream source permissions need clarification. English/generic/ATC content can support evaluation of a Romanian ingredient crosswalk; local market completeness and Romanian translation are unverified. The provider explicitly warns of errors/incompleteness and that missing interactions do not mean none exist. [Terms](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/terms/).

CC BY-NC-SA permits copying/adaptation subject to attribution, non-commercial use, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Adapted data may need its own compatible licensing and distribution; InterMED's Apache-2.0 code licence does not relicense the dataset. Store/distribution restrictions, donations/sponsorship and clinical use need review in the intended context. [CC conditions](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

### Liverpool HIV and HEP

**Content/quality:** specialist treatment-versus-comedication assessments, referenced mechanisms, management and predictions where direct studies are lacking. HIV and hepatitis treatment scope is valuable, but does not cover arbitrary comedication-versus-comedication pairs. Generic naming is visible; a licensed native-ID export and ATC/formal INN/RxNorm/UNII crosswalk are unverified. [HIV checker](https://www.hiv-druginteractions.org/checker), [HEP checker](https://www.hep-druginteractions.org/checker).

**Vocabulary/cadence:** Do Not Coadminister, Potential Interaction, Potential Weak Interaction, No Interaction Expected are risk categories, not evidence grades. Obtain the current evidence-quality dictionary separately; no complete enum set is claimed here. Dated site updates demonstrate ongoing revision, not a contracted monthly snapshot SLA. [HIV news](https://www.hiv-druginteractions.org/site_updates), [HEP news](https://www.hep-druginteractions.org/site_updates).

**Romania/rights:** relevant European treatments and generic names make these useful specialist adjuncts; complete Romanian coverage/localization is unverified. Public pages link some partner translations, without establishing Romanian rights. A public machine feed was not verified. Provider apps do not license redistribution into another app. Both sites require prior written consent for further reuse/distribution and retain notices. [HIV terms](https://www.hiv-druginteractions.org/terms), [HEP terms](https://www.hep-druginteractions.org/terms). Not shortlisted as a general DDI replacement because of scope and rights.

### openFDA drug labels and DailyMed SPL

**Content:** product-label narratives, including interaction text, rather than normalized pair rules or consistent severity/evidence enums. SPL document identity/version and product/ingredient codes support provenance, but ATC and authoritative INN normalization are not established by those fields. DailyMed publishes mapping resources including RxNorm; verify UNII/ingredient availability and missing mappings in the actual SPL/openFDA schema before designing joins. [openFDA label fields](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/label/searchable-fields/), [DailyMed mapping files](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/app-support-mapping-files.cfm), [DailyMed services](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/app-support-web-services.cfm).

**Cadence/quality:** openFDA labels update weekly; DailyMed offers daily/weekly/monthly update files. Submitted/in-use labels may differ from the latest FDA-approved label, and NLM does not review SPL before publication. openFDA explicitly warns against relying on its data for medical-care decisions. These limits prevent treating the API as a clinically approved DDI engine. [openFDA overview/warning](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/label/), [DailyMed overview](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/about-dailymed.cfm), [bulk files](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/spl-resources-all-drug-labels.cfm).

**Romania/rights:** predominantly US/English product information; local authorization, formulations and wording can differ. Potential supporting provenance only, with clinical review and appropriate permissions. openFDA's CC0 default has third-party exceptions; NLM's government-work policy is not a universal worldwide licence for all privately contributed labels. [openFDA licence](https://open.fda.gov/license/), [terms](https://open.fda.gov/terms/), [NLM copyright](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/web_policies.html#copyright).

### EMA SmPC / EPAR section 4.5

**Content/identifiers:** regulatory product information has interaction narratives in SmPC section 4.5, with related dosing, contraindication, warning and pharmacokinetic information elsewhere. EMA downloadable medicine metadata is not a structured DDI feed. Product/active-substance names and authorization metadata can support joins, but a complete normalized ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII interaction crosswalk is unverified. [SmPC guidance](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/marketing-authorisation/product-information-requirements/how-prepare-review-summary-product-characteristics), [medicine downloads](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/download-medicine-data).

**Vocabulary/cadence/quality:** authoritative authorization-specific recommendations, not a uniform pairwise severity/evidence taxonomy. Product information changes during the authorization lifecycle; no synchronized offline DDI snapshot SLA is established. The older interaction guideline explains inclusion and cross-references but is not the current standalone implementation standard: EMA lists ICH M12 and its EU implementation strategy, plus separate gastrointestinal interaction guidance under development. Assess current guidance rather than silently applying Revision 1. [Historical guideline, section 7](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/scientific-guideline/guideline-investigation-drug-interactions-revision-1_en.pdf), [current ICH M12 guidance and transition documents](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/scientific-guidelines/ich-m12-drug-interaction-studies).

**Romania/rights:** EU centrally authorized medicines and Romanian product information are relevant, but EPARs do not cover every nationally authorized Romanian product. EMA's acknowledgement-based reuse permission excludes third-party content; verify document ownership rather than assuming every hosted annex has identical rights. [Legal notice](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/about-us/about-website/legal-notice). See the fallback decision below.

### Romania: ANMDMR and Romanian-language references

**Content/identifiers:** the Nomenclator exposes Romanian DCI, ATC, CIM product identifiers, forms/strengths, authorization holders and RCP/prospect links. CIM is product identity; DCI requires reviewed ingredient/salt/combination normalization. RxNorm/UNII mappings are unverified. **Cadence:** the catalogue displayed an update of **2026-10-08** when assessed; individual RCP revision dates need separate tracking. **Vocabulary:** RCP section 4.5 is narrative, with no verified structured severity/evidence feed. [Official Nomenclator](https://nomenclator.anm.ro/medicamente).

**Fit/quality:** strongest direct Romanian regulatory/language alignment in this comparison, but not a standalone comprehensive DDI dataset. Catalogue updates do not prove all attached RCPs were revised together. The all-rights-reserved notice and lack of a verified device licence are blockers to blanket redistribution; an export button is not a licence.

Mediately's Romanian service is an example of a Romanian-language reference with end-user terms, not an approved reusable feed. Its [EULA](https://mediately.co/ro/docs/eula) does not establish InterMED offline rights. Existing repository policy forbids copying/scraping Mediately. No other reusable Romanian-language structured DDI dataset with verified device rights was established in this bounded assessment; this is not a claim that none exists. Ask ANMDMR and qualified Romanian clinicians about licensed local references and rights holders rather than reproducing books/apps.

### EU national example: France ANSM / public medicines database

**Content/vocabulary:** ANSM's interaction thesaurus uses four management constraints: contre-indication, association déconseillée, précaution d'emploi, à prendre en compte. These are not a separate graded evidence scale. It contains named substances/classes, not a verified ATC/INN/RxNorm/UNII machine crosswalk. **Critical freshness limit:** the current page identifies the final edition as **2023-09-15**, says maintenance has ended and announces availability until **2027-06-15**. Do not present it as a maintained production provider. [Official thesaurus status](https://ansm.sante.fr/documents/reference/thesaurus-des-interactions-medicamenteuses-1).

The French public medicines database supplies downloadable catalogue/composition files with CIS/CIP identifiers and a published reuse licence. Its page describes monthly files and intervening alerts. Those files are not a DDI rule feed, and their licence cannot automatically be extended to every linked RCP. [Downloads/licence](https://base-donnees-publique.medicaments.gouv.fr/telechargement), [ANSM legal notice](https://ansm.sante.fr/page/mentions-legales).

**Romania/limits:** French/EU regulatory terminology can support expert comparison, but French products, language and historical class rules cannot establish Romanian completeness. National regulator material is useful only with artifact-specific rights and current source dates. This example is not an exhaustive survey of every EU national agency.

## Ranked shortlist for a small non-commercial offline-first app

The ranking is a judgement about **which acquisition/clarification path to investigate first**, balancing rights feasibility, clinical detail, Romanian crosswalk potential and operational burden. It is not a ranking of demonstrated clinical accuracy or known price. All three fail at least one current adoption gate.

## Is SmPC section 4.5 a legally safe fallback?

**There is no blanket safe-fallback conclusion.** EMA's legal notice gives meaningful permission for EMA-owned material with acknowledgement, but excludes third-party content. ANMDMR access and official status do not establish a general reuse licence. Copyright, database extraction rights, translation/adaptation rights and distribution terms must be resolved for the actual documents and operations. This assessment identifies the rights questions; it does not approve a legal exemption.

Distinguish three proposals:

1. **Link to the current official RCP/SmPC:** avoids bundling its text, subject to applicable linking terms, but fails the offline requirement when the document is unavailable locally. Links still need product/version integrity.
2. **Ship approved documents or attributed excerpts offline:** potentially feasible for material with verified reproduction/distribution permission; retain notices, source, authorization, revision date and language. Check third-party content and any translation rights separately. A permitted excerpt is not automatically permission to rebuild a full database.
3. **Extract and normalize section 4.5 into pair rules:** adds database/adaptation rights questions and clinical interpretation. A licence to read a PDF, or public availability, does not establish this right. Qualified clinical review is required even where reuse is permitted.

The clinical limits also remain:

- Section 4.5 is product-specific narrative, not an exhaustive pairwise interaction graph. Class language, negative study findings, dose, route, timing, disease, renal/hepatic status and population qualifiers can change interpretation.
- Relevant advice may also be in sections 4.2, 4.3, 4.4 and 5.2. Reading section 4.5 alone can discard the controlling contraindication, dose adjustment or mechanism context. [EMA SmPC guidance](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/marketing-authorisation/product-information-requirements/how-prepare-review-summary-product-characteristics).
- There is no universal severity/evidence enum across SmPCs. Opposite product labels can differ in detail and revision date; omissions cannot become “no interaction”. Approval-specific text is authoritative for that product's terms, but is not a validated multi-product alert engine.
- Regulatory updates are not synchronized across all documents. An offline package needs a verified revision inventory, update monitoring, recall/correction route and stale-content policy.
- Translation, summarization and extraction can alter meaning. No LLM-generated verdict or automated severity assignment can replace permitted source content and clinician review.

**Proposed fallback:** only a bounded, rights-cleared, clinically reviewed regulatory-information feature showing source documents/excerpts and explicit coverage limits, if the owner approves that scope. It must not claim comprehensive DDI checking and must not be counted as approval of the M10 provider gate. Prefer current Romanian RCPs for local product context when rights permit; EMA central product information alone is incomplete for the national market.
